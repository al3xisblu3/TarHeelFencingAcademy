About Our Academy

Located in the Fetzer Building at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, our fencing Academy is dedicated to teaching the ancient art of fencing to fencers of all skill levels.

Our experienced coaches and welcoming community make us the perfect place to start your fencing journey or take your skills to the next level.

Meet Our Coaches

Adam Kaszubowski

Foil • Epee • Sabre

USA Fencing certified | 30+ years in fencing • Coach & Referee

Adam Kaszubowski has been involved with fencing for more than 30 years, first as an athlete and then as a coach and referee. He was a bronze individual and team gold medalist at the 1994 Polish Cadet Championships, as well as a silver team medalist at both the 1996 and 1997 Polish Junior Championships. In July 2019, Kaszubowski joined the Penn State University fencing team, which took team runner-up at the 2021 NCAA Championships. Under his leadership at PSU, his athletes achieved nine All-American honors. In October 2024, Adam joined the UNC Fencing program. Championships: 1st place in Algiers 2016, Cairo 2015, Cairo 2014, and Cape Town 2013.
