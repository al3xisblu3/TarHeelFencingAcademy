About Tar Heels Fencing Academy

Club Overview

Tar Heels Fencing Academy is home to driven athletes who love the fast-paced thrill of fencing. With high-quality coaching and a training philosophy rooted in discipline, technique, and tactical excellence, we prepare fencers to compete—and succeed—at local, regional, and national levels. Our athletes benefit from structured programs, focused instruction, and a team culture that pushes each member to reach new heights. At Tar Heels Fencing Academy, we train champions in both skill and character.

Location & Facilities

Fetzer Building, UNC Chapel Hill, NC.

Membership & Programs

We offer a clear pathway for fencers at every level—with structured progression, dedicated coaching, and options to fit your goals.

  • Introductory Program — Monday & Wednesday, 5:30–6:30 PM. Fundamentals, footwork, blade control, and bout basics in a supportive group setting.
  • Competitive Program — Monday–Wednesday, 6:00–8:00 PM. Advanced technique and tactics, conditioning, and bout strategy for athletes pursuing competition.
  • Private Lessons — 20- or 40-minute one-on-one sessions tailored to the athlete’s needs (48-hour cancellation policy).
  • Clinics & Camps — Periodic training opportunities with elite coaches for focused development across ages and skill levels.

See our Schedule for current session times and seasonal updates.
